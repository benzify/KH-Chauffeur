Confirmed answers

Know what is included—and what still needs confirmation.

How much are the Mercedes Sprinters?

Both confirmed 14-passenger Sprinter options are $195 per hour. The minimum is five hours Friday–Saturday and four hours Sunday–Thursday.

What is the minimum for the limousine bus?

The 28-passenger limousine bus is $250 per hour with a five-hour minimum, producing a $1,250 base minimum.

What are the SUV rates?

The Cadillac Escalade ESV is $150 per hour with a four-hour minimum. The Mercedes-Benz G-Wagon is $200 per hour with a four-hour minimum. Passenger capacities are pending.

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit is required to reserve after availability and terms are confirmed. The remaining 50% base balance is due upon arrival.

Are tolls and parking included?

They are included for Metro Atlanta trips and additional for out-of-town trips. The exact boundary and additional-charge calculation are pending.

Does the website provide a final quote?

No. It provides an estimated base rental from confirmed hourly rates and minimums. Availability and any applicable charges must be confirmed.

Does submitting the form reserve a vehicle?

No. It prepares an email inquiry. A reservation is confirmed only through an approved availability, terms and deposit process.

What policies are still pending?

Taxes, gratuity status, mileage or travel-time charges, overtime increments, holiday rules, cancellation, refund, rescheduling and no-show terms have not been approved.

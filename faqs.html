Rates and seating

How much are the Mercedes Sprinters?

Both confirmed 14-passenger Sprinter options are $195 per hour. The minimum is five hours Friday–Saturday and four hours Sunday–Thursday.

What is the minimum for the limousine bus?

The 28-passenger limousine bus is $250 per hour with a five-hour minimum, producing a $1,250 base minimum.

What are the SUV rates?

The Cadillac Escalade ESV is $150 per hour with a four-hour minimum. The Mercedes-Benz G‑Wagon is $200 per hour with a four-hour minimum. Share your group size in your request and the team will confirm seating.

How many passengers fit in each vehicle?

Both Sprinters seat up to 14 passengers and the limousine bus seats up to 28. For the Cadillac Escalade ESV and Mercedes-Benz G‑Wagon, include your group size in your request and the team will confirm seating.

Deposit and charges

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit is required to reserve after availability and terms are confirmed. The remaining 50% base balance is due upon arrival.

What isn't included in the estimate?

The estimate covers the base rental only. Taxes, gratuity, mileage or travel time, overtime and holiday rates aren't added; the team confirms any that apply. Ask the team about cancellation and rescheduling terms.

Trips and service area

Which areas do you serve?

Atlanta, McDonough and surrounding areas, up to roughly 30–40 miles away. Share your full route in your request and the team will confirm it. See the service area.

Are tolls and parking included?

They are included for Metro Atlanta trips and additional for out-of-town trips. Share your full route in your request so the team can confirm how it is treated.

Estimates and requests

How do I get an estimate?

Each vehicle page and the request form calculate an estimated base rental from your hours and the day's minimum, with the 50% deposit and the balance due on arrival.

Does the website provide a final quote?

No. It provides an estimated base rental from confirmed hourly rates and minimums. Availability and any applicable charges must be confirmed.

Does submitting the form reserve a vehicle?

No. It prepares an email inquiry. A reservation is confirmed only through an approved availability, terms and deposit process.

How a request works.

Four steps from choosing a vehicle to a confirmed booking. Nothing is reserved until the team confirms it.

  1. Choose a vehicle

    Compare hourly rates, passenger counts and minimums above or on the fleet page.

  2. Build an estimate

    Each vehicle page and the request form apply the right minimum for your day and show the 50% deposit.

  3. Send your inquiry

    The request form prepares an email with your date, route, group size and hours.

  4. Confirm with the team

    The team confirms availability and any other charges. A 50% deposit reserves the vehicle; the balance is due on arrival.
